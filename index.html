MURIQ-AI SECURITY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 08, 2026

Welcome to Muriq-AI. Your financial privacy is our highest priority. This document outlines how we handle your information and secure your data.

1. Data Collection & Cloud Sync

Muriq-AI collects only the data necessary to provide professional financial insights. This includes transactions, account names, and categories you manually input. We do not access your bank accounts directly. To provide seamless cross-device synchronization, your data is securely stored in your personal cloud account.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide core functionality, Muriq-AI utilizes the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, real-time database syncing, and cloud storage (profile photos). Your data is strictly linked to your account and protected by Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Google Gemini API: Used exclusively for processing AI receipt scanning and categorization. Data sent for processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and is not retained for training public AI models.

3. Biometric Security & Device Data

Our app utilizes device-level Fingerprint and Face Unlock mechanisms to secure your data and prevent unauthorized account deletions.

Important: All biometric data processing occurs 100% locally on your device. We NEVER access, collect, or upload your biometric data (fingerprints or face scans) to our servers or Firebase.

4. Camera, Gallery & Microphone

We request Camera, Gallery (Storage), and Microphone access for distinct features:

  1. Profile Photos: If you upload a custom profile picture, that image is securely stored in your personal cloud storage to sync across devices.
  2. AI Receipt Scanning & Voice Input: Images of receipts and voice inputs are temporarily processed via the Google Gemini API to extract financial data. These media files are NOT stored permanently on any server and are discarded immediately after processing.

5. Data Security & Sharing

We use industry-standard encryption for all data synchronization and storage. Your information is strictly private. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing agencies under any circumstances.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. To protect you from accidental or unauthorized deletion, we have a multi-step verification process (including Biometric validation and Account Re-authentication).

What gets deleted: All transactions, accounts, categories, cloud backup data, uploaded profile photos, and your authentication record are permanently and irreversibly removed.

To initiate account deletion: Go to Settings → Scroll down → "Delete Account & All Data".

7. Data Retention

Your data remains securely stored in the cloud as long as your account is active, ensuring you can restore it upon your next login. Data is only permanently deleted when you explicitly complete the Account Deletion process.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Muriq-AI is a professional financial tool and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Contact Support

For any privacy-related inquiries, please reach out to us at: muriqapp@gmail.com
