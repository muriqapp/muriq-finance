Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

Welcome to Muriq-AI. Your financial privacy is our highest priority. This document outlines how we handle your information and secure your data.

1. Data Collection

Muriq-AI collects only the data necessary to provide professional financial insights. This includes transactions, account names, and categories you manually input. We do not access your bank accounts directly.

2. Camera & Gallery Access

We request camera and gallery (storage) access solely for the AI Receipt Scanning feature. Images are temporarily processed via the Google Gemini API to extract financial data. These images are NOT stored permanently on any server and are deleted immediately after processing. We do not use your camera or gallery for any other purpose.

3. Microphone Access

We request microphone access solely for the AI Voice Input feature, which allows you to add transactions and interact with the app hands-free. Voice data is processed in real-time and is never stored on any server or shared with third parties.

4. AI & Image Processing

When you use the AI Receipt Scanning feature, images are temporarily processed via the Google Gemini API to extract financial data. These images are NOT stored permanently on any server and are deleted immediately after processing.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for all data synchronization. Your information is stored privately and is never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

6. User Rights

You have the absolute right to export, modify, or delete your entire financial history at any time through the app settings.

7. Contact Support

For any privacy-related inquiries, please reach out to us at: muriqapp@gmail.com
